1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy covers Healthy Life Scale ("we," "us," "this site") and all of its tools, which are hosted across the healthylifescale.com domain and its subdomains (for example bmi.healthylifescale.com, symptoms.healthylifescale.com, mentalhealth.healthylifescale.com, and the others linked in the menu above). One policy applies to all of them.

By using any tool on this site, you agree to the practices described here. If you don't agree, please don't use the site.

2. Information we don't collect

There are no user accounts anywhere on this site. We do not ask for your name, email address, or any other directly identifying information to use a calculator. The health details you enter into a tool — your weight, blood pressure reading, symptoms, cycle dates, mood-survey answers, and so on — are not transmitted to or stored on our servers. We simply don't have a database of your entries.

3. Data stored on your device

Some tools include an optional history or trend feature — for example, tracking your BMI over time, logging sleep, saving period dates, or recording breath-holding attempts. Where that exists, it works using your browser's built-in local storage, not a server:

  • The data is saved only inside your browser, on your device.
  • It is never sent to us, to Google, or to anyone else.
  • It stays until you clear your browser data, use a different browser/device, or the tool provides its own "reset" option.
  • If you switch devices or browsers, that history won't carry over, because it was never centrally stored.

The Symptom Checker in particular does not save your selections anywhere, including locally — each visit starts fresh.

4. Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand overall site traffic — things like which tools are popular, which pages people leave from, approximate location at a country/city level (derived from IP address, which Google may truncate or anonymize), device and browser type, and referring pages. This is aggregate usage data, not the health information you type into a calculator.

Google Analytics uses cookies to do this. You can read about how Google handles this data in Google's Privacy Policy, and you can opt out of Google Analytics tracking site-wide using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Advertising

This site is set up to display ads served through Google AdSense. Once ads are live, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads (including personalized ads based on your browsing activity) and to measure ad performance. This can involve sharing some data (such as your approximate location and browsing behavior on this and other sites) with Google and third-party ad vendors.

You can control personalized advertising from Google at Google Ads Settings, and learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services at this Google page.

6. Other third-party services

A few tools load resources from other providers to work correctly:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) — used to display the site's typefaces. Loading a font can expose your IP address to Google in the same way loading any web resource does.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) — the Symptom Checker's interactive 3D body map loads the Three.js graphics library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com.

These providers may log basic request data (like IP address and browser type) as part of normal web infrastructure; we don't control their data practices and encourage you to review their own privacy policies if you'd like more detail.

7. Cookies & your choices

Cookies used on this site fall into two categories: analytics cookies (Google Analytics, described above) and, once active, advertising cookies (Google AdSense, described above). We don't set our own first-party tracking cookies beyond what's needed for these services.

You can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser's settings. Doing so may not break any tool's core functionality, since the calculators themselves don't require cookies to work — blocking cookies will just stop analytics/ad personalization.

8. Children's privacy

This site is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we don't collect accounts or personal identifiers from anyone, we don't knowingly hold any child's personal data.

9. Not medical advice or a medical record

Nothing you enter into a Healthy Life Scale tool becomes part of a medical record, and nothing on this site is medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. These tools provide general, educational estimates only. Always talk to a qualified healthcare provider about your health, symptoms, or any results a tool gives you. In an emergency, contact your local emergency number immediately rather than using this site.

10. Your rights

Because we don't operate accounts or store your calculator entries on our servers, there's generally no personal data on our side for you to request, correct, or delete — the data you'd want control over (your BMI history, sleep log, symptom selections, etc.) already lives entirely on your own device, under your control, via your browser settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws like the EU/UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) regarding data processed by our analytics and advertising partners. You can exercise many of these directly with Google using the opt-out links in sections 4 and 5.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example as tools change or as we enable advertising. We'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page when we do. Significant changes will be reflected here; we encourage you to check back periodically.

About Healthy Life Scale

Healthy Life Scale is a collection of free health and wellness calculators — a calorie and macro tracker, BMI calculator, water intake calculator, TDEE calculator, sleep calculator, pregnancy due date and weight gain calculators, period and ovulation tracker, child height predictor, blood pressure calculator, heart age calculator, breath holding test, ASCVD heart risk calculator, symptom checker, and a PHQ-9/GAD-7 mental health survey. Every tool is free to use, works without an account or app download, and saves your data privately on your own device rather than a server. Built for anyone who wants quick, practical health calculators without ads, logins, or paywalls.
