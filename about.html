About Healthy Life Scale

Free tools. No account. Your data stays with you.

What this is

Healthy Life Scale is a small collection of free, focused health and wellness calculators — currently 17 tools covering everything from BMI and calorie tracking to blood pressure, heart age, sleep, pregnancy, fertility, mental health screening, and a symptom urgency checker.

Each tool is built to do one thing well: take a few real inputs and give you a clear, useful answer in seconds — grounded in established formulas and guidelines (things like the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, AHA blood pressure categories, IOM pregnancy weight guidance, the ACC/AHA ASCVD risk calculator, and the validated PHQ-9 / GAD-7 screening instruments) rather than anything invented or proprietary.

Why it's built this way

🔒 Private by default
There are no accounts and nothing is stored on a server. If a tool tracks history or trends (like a BMI log or sleep tracker), that data lives only in your browser's local storage, on your device.
⚡ Fast, no friction
No sign-up, no email gate, no app download. Open a tool, enter your numbers, get an answer.
💰 Free to use
Every tool is free. The site may eventually show ads to help cover hosting costs, but the tools themselves are never paywalled.
📜 Grounded in guidelines
Each calculator is built on established, public medical/scientific formulas and guidelines, referenced on its page — not on made-up scoring.

What this isn't

This site is not a medical provider, and none of these tools diagnose, treat, or replace a clinician. They're meant to help you understand your numbers and decide how urgently to seek care — not to tell you what condition you have. Always talk to a qualified healthcare provider about symptoms, test results, medications, or anything you're concerned about.

In a medical emergency, don't rely on this site or any online tool — call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency room right away.

About Healthy Life Scale

Healthy Life Scale is a collection of free health and wellness calculators — a calorie and macro tracker, BMI calculator, water intake calculator, TDEE calculator, sleep calculator, pregnancy due date and weight gain calculators, period and ovulation tracker, child height predictor, blood pressure calculator, heart age calculator, breath holding test, ASCVD heart risk calculator, symptom checker, and a PHQ-9/GAD-7 mental health survey. Every tool is free to use, works without an account or app download, and saves your data privately on your own device rather than a server. Built for anyone who wants quick, practical health calculators without ads, logins, or paywalls.
