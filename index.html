How it works

  1. Search or add a food. Type into the search box to pick from 240+ common foods, or use "Add a custom food" for anything not listed, with your own numbers.
  2. Confirm the quantity. Adjust the amount to match what you actually ate before adding it to your log.
  3. Watch your totals update. The Nutrition Facts panel at the top automatically sums everything you've logged that day.
  4. Set daily goals (optional). Tap the target icon to set a calorie and protein goal — the panel shows how much you have left.
  5. Browse past days. Use the arrows above the panel to look back at any previous day's log.

Everything is saved locally on your own device — there's no account, no server, and nothing is shared.
